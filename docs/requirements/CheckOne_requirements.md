## 2. Product

Bench test fixture ("jiga") for production. It powers up to eight GapOne / FluxOne sensors with 24 V, measures
each 4–20 mA loop, converts it to the sensor's quantity and shows it on a 3.5" display and on a web page served by
the fixture; it also monitors the sensor supply (voltage and current) and two NTC temperatures, and logs everything
with date and time (DS 1). Not a product for sale; up to five units on one network (CheckOne1 … CheckOne5, DS 4.2).

## 5. Temperature, clock, bus

- **NTC:** two inputs, 100 kΩ / B3950, XH connectors: NTC1 on the front (probe, cable 1.35 m, up to 150 °C), NTC2
  inside. 10 kΩ divider from 3V3A, the input protection of the loops (1 kΩ, BAT54S, 10 nF) and 1 µF across the NTC
  at the connector (≈ 17 Hz). Third ADS1115 (0x4A): NTC1 AIN0, 3V3A AIN1 (ratiometric), NTC2 AIN2. Beta equation;
  Steinhart-Hart with a 2–3 point calibration for 140 °C probes (DS 5.3). Divider 0.55 V at 150 °C (calc 2).
- **RTC:** PCF8563 (0x51), 32.768 kHz crystal, CR2032 through 2 × BAT54W; UTC−3; NTP (pool.ntp.br) or web (DS 5.4).
- **I²C:** 400 kHz, 4.7 kΩ pull-ups, polled (no interrupt lines); on the CYD CN1 GPIO21 SDA / GPIO22 SCL (DS 5.5).
  Addresses 0x40, 0x48–0x4A, 0x51; no conflict with the 4.3" display's GT911 (0x5D / 0x14) or codec (0x18) (DS 7.5).

## 6. Displays and firmware (hardware-relevant part)

- Three display modules, **one connected at a time** (both are I²C masters on the same bus) (DS 7.5):
  CYD ESP32-3248S035R (ESP32, 4 MB flash, no PSRAM, ST7796 or ILI9488, resistive touch, inside the box);
  Guition JC3248W535 (ESP32-S3, 16 MB / 8 MB PSRAM, capacitive, inside the box, other cover cut-out and spacers);
  Guition JC4880P443C 4.3" (ESP32-P4 + C6, outside the box, 4-wire cable to J_EXT).
- The processor is the display module's ESP32; firmware ESP-IDF 5.5 + LVGL, web page embedded, OTA with two app
  partitions; configuration in NVS (DS 5.6). Watchdog, brown-out, I²C bus recovery, missing-device detection.
- CYD supply: its LDO needs ~4.4 V; through the SS34 and beads it gets ~4.5 V (4.3 V worst case): fit the 0 Ω with
  the CYD and do not plug the display's USB with the fixture powered (DS 7.5).
- Logging: one sample every 5 s, ~28 B, 48 h (~1 MB); PSRAM ring on the JC modules, flash partition written in small
  pages about once a minute on the CYD (DS 6, 9).
- Start-up: ignore channel readings for 2–3 s after power-up and while the bus is below 20 V (DS 9).

## 8. Assembly and parts

JLCPCB assembles the SMD parts (DS 8.1, with JLCPCB codes and Basic / Extended class); the THT parts (terminals,
DC jack, XH, J_EXT header, battery holder) are bought from LCSC and hand-soldered, with the loose items (DS 8.2).
The quantities of DS 8.1 agree with the circuit described in DS 5 (checked 2026-11-16).

## 11. Gaps

- G-1 The ngspice netlists and results (`docs/simulacao/`) are not in the hand-over.
- G-2 No datasheet on disk: `docs/datasheets/` is empty; V1 symbols and footprints were drawn from datasheets that
  were not kept (V1R).
- G-3 Sensor current consumption (GapOne, FluxOne) not stated; the budget uses 75 mA from the screen mock-up data.
- G-4 Sheet title blocks of V1 carry rev 1.0 and no date; the board silkscreen says "rev 1.0".
- G-5 V1 footprint issues: `Package_SO:VSSOP-10_3x3mm_P0.5mm` (ADS1115, INA226) is not in the KiCad 10 library;
  footprints carry no LCSC field and no library nickname on the board (DRC schematic parity: 360 items); V1R item 8
  (BAT54W package, FCH1040 pads, MX1.25 connector) still to check.
- G-7 The zip "for Claude Code to generate the display firmware" (MT) is not in the hand-over.
- G-8 No drawing or measured inside dimensions of the AK-P-08A on disk (the 3D viewer PL shows the enclosure but
  carries no dimensions in text): needed to check the board fit and the holes (MT request 3).
- G-6 The items DS 11 lists to confirm on real parts: CYD size and holes, window wall depth vs the 12.5 mm
  terminal body, ribs in the windows, sensors' start-up current vs the 200 mA PTCs, CYD display controller,
  AQTech logo SVG, JC4880P443C 26-pin pinout, SMAJ5.0A leakage at 2.5 V.
