Description                                                                                                         Breakdown Voltage
                                                                                                                          3.3 to 440 V
The SMBJ series is designed specifically to protect sensitive electronic
equipment from voltage transients induced by lightning and other transient                                           Peak Pulse Power
voltage events                                                                                                                600 W

Features

·For surface mounted applications in order to optimize board space                                                  DO-214AA(SMB)
·Low leakage
·Glass passivated junction
·Low inductance
·Excellent clamping capability
·600W peak pulse power capability at 10/1000s waveform
·Fast response time
·Typical IR less than 5A above 12V
·High Temperature soldering: 260 /40 seconds at terminals
·Typical maximum temperature coefficient VBR = 0.1% ×VBR@25 ×T
·Plastic package has Underwriters Laboratory Flammability 94V-0
·Matte tin lead­free Plated
·Halogen free and RoHS compliant
·Typical failure mode is short from over-specified voltage or current
·Whisker test is conducted based on JEDEC JESD201A per its table 4a and 4c
·IEC 61000-4-2 ESD 30KV(Air),30KV(contact)

  Applications

TVS devices are ideal for the protection of I/O interfaces, VCC bus and other vulnerable circuits used in Telecom, Computer,
Industrial and Consumer electronic applications
