# Pitfalls (each one happened on a real board)

| Symptom | Cause | What the skill does now |
|---|---|---|
| The prompt said "all parts on top" while a note on F.Fab said "B side: AFE, ADC, supplies" | Instructions written without the mechanical notes | `board_survey.py` prints every note on non-copper layers; a conflict is a question, not a choice |
| The old layout looked like a good guide for zones | It was a proposal with parts inside the battery holder and on top of each other | Treat any previous layout as a hint; check it for overlaps first |
| A feasible board looked full: 21 test points found no room with 1150 mm² "free" | Occupancy raster of 0.25 mm rounded every part and halo outward (+40 % area); the free area was slivers | Fine raster (0.05 mm) for occupancy, 0.25 mm grid only for positions |
| Whole blocks spilled to the other side of the board | Regions sized by eye; utilisation above 0.8 in two regions while others sat at 0.3 | `place.py --budget` per block; rebalance before placing |
| No decoupling capacitor was recognised at all | Pads in the `.kicad_pcb` have no pin type | Pin types and functions from the exported netlist (`extract_schematic.py`) |
| Op-amp V− capacitors were treated as "ground" capacitors | Pin names like `V-` matched a ground pattern | Grounds = pins named GND/VSS/EP only; rails = power pin types or supply-like names |
| Bulk capacitors of a rail were pulled across the board to a module | Any IC on the rail counted | Pairing by schematic distance on the same sheet, with a limit |
| Capacitors 5–13 mm from their IC | IC placed first with its power pins against an edge or another IC | Capacitors placed right after their IC; then two improvement passes (evict small parts near the pin; re-place IC + capacitors in 4 rotations) |
| Parts ended 0.91 mm from a chamfered edge | Edge mask computed on the coarse grid with too little margin | Edge mask with one grid step of extra inset; `verify_placement.py` measures the real distance |
| A git/line diff showed 7000 changed lines | KiCad reorders footprint items on rotation | `compare_boards.py` compares content by UUID: proves "placement only" |
| `pcbnew.FootprintLoad` returned an untyped object | API inconsistency | Footprints loaded through `PCB_IO_MGR.FindPlugin(KICAD_SEXP)` |
| `GetBoardPolygonOutlines`, `Flip`, `Inflate` signatures differ between KiCad 8, 9 and 10 | API changes | Compatibility wrappers in `placement_core.py` |
| 199 `silk_over_copper` warnings after a dense placement | Designators on neighbouring pads | `hide_silk_refs.py` (field kept, hidden); report it as silkscreen, not placement |
| The board title did not fit anywhere on the full top side | Density | `find_clear_spot.py`: other side, smaller (≥ 1.0 mm) or two lines - ask before shrinking |
| The placement board was open in KiCad while scripts rewrote it | Two writers | Write to a new file; tell the user to reload before saving in KiCad |
| Courtyard "overlaps" reported on a production board that DRC passed | Bounding boxes of rotated or L-shaped courtyards overlap while the polygons do not | `verify_placement.py` measures the exact polygon gap (boolean intersection + vertex distance) after a bounding-box prefilter |
| The spacing check failed 371 pairs on a board that was built and works | That fab and team place courtyards 0.1–0.2 mm apart; 0.3 mm is a default, not a law | `spacing` is a plan parameter: take it from the user, the fab rules or the board's own practice, and state it in the report |
| Logo and artwork footprints counted as parts to place, or as obstacles | Footprints without pads | Padless footprints are treated as fixed artwork, ignored by the spacing check, and their bounding box on the front layers is a keep-out (ten parts were placed over a front-side logo before this) |
| Parts placed inside a keep-out drawn on the board | Rule areas in the `.kicad_pcb` were not read | Rule areas that forbid footprints become keep-outs in placer and verifier |
| `board.Zones()` / `Tracks()` returned untyped SWIG objects, attribute errors | Containers iterated after `board.Remove()` | Collect every item first, then remove; read zones through `GetArea(i)` |
| The draft plan put every part in the catch-all block | Schematic positions keyed by the sheet FILE stem ("untitled", "sensor") while the board carries the sheet INSTANCE name ("Sensor 13-24") | `extract_schematic.py` takes the instance name from the netlist `sheetpath`; `select` accepts either |
| The last block of the draft plan got 100 mm² for 530 mm² of parts; two blocks sharing a region spilled 38 parts | Regions handed out at a fixed 60 % utilisation, and a shared rectangle is filled by whichever block is placed first | `make_plan.py` scales the target utilisation to the real total, picks a block's extra regions near its first one, and cuts a shared region into one slice per block |
| An IC's capacitors 14–17 mm away; a fixed IC's capacitors 10 mm away | IC position chosen without room for its capacitors; capacitors of fixed ICs placed after the blocks filled the space | IC and capacitors placed together (best of k IC positions × 4 rotations); capacitors of fixed ICs placed first |
| A hot resistor 2.6 mm from an ADC | Thermal separation only checked afterwards | `heat` / `heat_sensitive` distance enforced while placing |
| A through-hole connector placed 0.46 mm from a fixed SMD transformer (1.0 mm required) | The occupancy carried only the halo of the part already placed; the halo of the part being placed was ignored | `place.py` tests each part inflated by (its halo − base spacing): the gap is at least the larger of the two halos |
| Four +5 V capacitors assigned to an isolated converter 66 mm away instead of the module that makes +5 V | The source module's output pin was typed `output` with function `+VOUT_6`; the leading sign defeated the name match | Pin functions are matched without a leading `+`/`-`; decoupling capacitors try a 8 mm, then a 16 mm window around the pin before the block |
| A draft plan for a 56 % board still spilled 28 parts | The regions covered only 80 % of the free area, so every block ran at 0.72 | `make_plan.py` cuts up to 24 regions (down to 3 % of free area left) and warns when the target utilisation exceeds 0.65 |
| DRC on the placed board reported edge-clearance errors on a fixed USB connector | The board was checked without the project's `.kicad_dru`, which holds the engineer's accepted exceptions | Copy `.kicad_pro` and `.kicad_dru` next to the new board; also run DRC on the original and report only the new errors |
| A 330 µF electrolytic 1.9 mm from a 6 W DC/DC module passed the thermal check | `heat_sensitive` held only the ICs the engineer listed; nobody listed the electrolytic | Aluminium electrolytics are heat-sensitive by default (`electrolytic_footprints`), GapOne CON 2026-10-30 |
| SMD side-view LEDs failed 1 mm iron-access spacing against their neighbours | An unnumbered plated light hole in the LED footprint made `is_tht` true | `is_tht` counts numbered plated holes only (1.2.0) |
| Peak-detector diodes 12-16 mm from their op-amp, a Sallen-Key capacitor 20 mm from its op-amp, ADC input resistors in another block - on a placement that passed every rule | The block placer pulls only decoupling capacitors to pins; signal passives land wherever the region has room | `signal_near.py` rules for signal passives (4 / 6 / 8 mm targets), GapOne CON 2026-11-05 (D-339) |
| Re-packing the passives with the ICs fixed barely helped (36 → 38 misses, sum of excess 239 → 175 mm) | The space at the pins was taken: decaps, a DC/DC module, parts of other blocks, and ICs turned with the wrong side toward their chain | Floorplan the analog ICs first (`ic_moves`: chain pin to pin, dual op-amps stacked by channel), then `pack_passives.py` - 22 misses, all within 3 mm of target |
| A greedy re-pack found "no free spot" in regions only 39-53 % full; then one block's parts filled another block's pin area | Greedy fragmentation, and blocks sharing free space | Regions per block, the most constrained parts first (decaps, then input-pin parts), relocation passes and pair swaps |
| A logo blocked a whole filter block in the re-pack | A padless artwork footprint on the BOTTOM side was treated as a top-side obstacle | Artwork is an obstacle only on the front layers (`artwork_keepouts`) |
| A placement that passed every rule still broke six datasheet layout rules found after routing: oscillator 24 mm from the ADC clock pin, ADC input capacitor 10 mm away, current monitor 41 mm from its shunt, USB series resistors 31-40 mm from the MCU | Spacing, edge and decoupling checks do not know which parts a datasheet wants together; the router then put the clock in one corridor with the analog inputs and ran sense lines along the power track | `near` entries in the plan (pad-to-pad maximum), checked by `verify_placement.py`; `make_plan.py` proposes candidates from the netlist (`_near_suggestions`). ServiceOne, 2026-10-05 |
| Generated candidates scored 24 % better than the manual placement and were no easier to route | The generator kept 0.15 mm between courtyards (median gap 0.16 mm) where the engineer keeps 0.55 mm; shorter ratsnest came from the squeeze | `run_placement.py --margin auto` measures the gap of the source placement; the gallery shows the typical gap of every candidate |
| "The placement leaves no room for vias" was reported as the cause of 40 unstitched plane pads | Not measured: the same stitcher skipped 39 pads on the engineer's own placement (IC ground pins; the tool models long pins as circles) | Measure a suspected placement defect on the original board before blaming the placement (`via_room_report.py`, routed baseline) |
| Every net of an isolated domain came back unrouted on all candidates | KiCad's Specctra export writes a placement-only rule area as a full keep-out | `route_candidates.py` removes placement-only rule areas from the routing copies |
| Four candidates routed at once were killed for lack of memory | Each Freerouting instance is memory hungry (32 GB exhausted) | One board at a time (`--parallel 1`) |
| A candidate passed its own scores and failed 16 of 30 datasheet decoupling checks | The generator knew nothing of the project's `layout_checks` | Decoupling entries of `kicad_golden.project.json` become pin pairs with a limit; the gallery counts pairs beyond it |
| Parts touched a keep-out band and a pad sat 0.38 mm from the edge | Overlaps smaller than half a raster cell were invisible | When optimising, the edge is inset by the copper-to-edge rule and keep-outs grow by one cell |
