## Findings

The conflicts between the description and v1, and the gaps, are in `docs/requirements/CheckOne_requirements.md`
(C-1…C-12, G-1…G-6) and the decisions they need in `CLAUDE.md` (O1…O8). The two that change the circuit most:
the display 5 V path as fitted in v1 (SS34 in, 0 Ω out) against the CYD's supply need (C-5), and the current that
+24 V on an Iin input pushes into the 3V3A rail through the BAT54S (C-12, `calc/checkone_budget.py` item 3).
