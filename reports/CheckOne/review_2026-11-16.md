# CheckOne v1 — electronics review (2026-11-16)

Request: S. Zimath via the owner (meeting 2026-11-16, item 1: "review the schematic and the whole electronics").
Inputs: `hardware/CheckOne/` (v1, generator `gerador/design.py`), the 26 datasheets fetched today into
`docs/datasheets/` (manifest), `docs/requirements/CheckOne_requirements.md`, `calc/checkone_budget.py`.

## Conclusion

The circuit is sound: every IC pinout, the I²C addresses, the buck and LDO application circuits, the protection
ratings and the measurement numbers check out against the datasheets. **Five things must change before the board is
ordered**; three of them are footprints that would make a part unusable, two are circuit choices.

| # | Severity | Finding | Proposal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | must fix | **D50, D51 BAT54W (C7502705) is SOD-123**, the board has SOT-323 pads | footprint `Diode_SMD:D_SOD-123`, symbol a plain Schottky (1 K, 2 A); same nets |
| 2 | must fix | **J1 DC-005-A200: the three slots are turned 90°** against XUNPU's recommended layout, and slot 3 is mirrored to the other side of the axis | redraw the footprint from the drawing (slots 2 and 4 across the jack axis, slot 3 along it, on the side the front view shows) |
| 3 | must fix | **KF2EDGRC-5.08-4P footprint uses 12.2 mm (the height) as the depth**; the body is 8.40 mm deep, the hole 1.30 mm from its back | correct the outline; the 8 terminals then end 3.8 mm further from the board edge than v1 and the 3D fit study show: move them or confirm with the enclosure (O9) |
| 4 | should fix | **Display 5 V as assembled: D4 SS34 fitted, R6 0 Ω DNP**; with the CYD (the display in use) this leaves its LDO at 4.3–4.5 V for ~4.4 V needed (C-5) | default assembly R6 fitted, D4 DNP; D4 only for the JC3248W535 |
| 5 | should fix | **+24 V on Iin pushes 2.8–3.8 mA per channel into 3V3A** through R1n1 1 kΩ and the BAT54S; the XC6206 cannot sink it (C-12, O4) | R1n1 1 kΩ → 4.7 kΩ (C23162, already in the BOM): 0.6–0.8 mA, input filter 3.4 kHz, gain error 0.11 % removed by the calibration; also halves the reversed-loop current in the lower diode |

## 3. Schematic and board hygiene (rev 1.1)

1. ERC: 5 `power_pin_not_driven` — add power flags on VIN_S, V24_F, +5V, +3V3A, VRTC / GND.
2. ERC: 6 `lib_symbol_mismatch` (KiCad 7 library copies) and 4 footprint links (finding table 1): refresh at the
   KiCad 10 conversion.
3. DRC: 360 schematic-parity items: board footprints carry no library nickname and no LCSC field.
4. DRC: LED1 1.15 mm from the board edge (copper_edge_clearance); J2 (NTC1) courtyard into J18 (CH8).
5. Test pads Ø1.5 (v1) vs Ø1.8 mm (description): pick one (C-7).
6. Title blocks: rev 1.0, no date, A2 → rev 1.1 with date on every sheet and the board (O8); English names (C-11).
7. Datasheet check: the schematic values are descriptive ("ADS1115 (0x48)", "CH1 +24/GND/Iin/Iout"), so the manifest
   (by part number) does not match them; at rev 1.1 the value becomes the part number. GZ2012D601TF (C1017) has no
   PDF at LCSC: to fetch from Sunlord.

## 4. Not verifiable yet
